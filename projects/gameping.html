Featured Project

GamePing

A no-login browser tool for gaming network diagnostics. It measures latency, jitter and packet-loss behavior and presents the results in a simple gaming-focused dashboard.

Launch GamePingGitHub

Why it exists

Gaming can feel laggy even when speed tests look great. GamePing focuses on the network signals that matter during real-time play.

Core diagnostics

Latency, jitter, packet loss, live samples, connection scoring and region selection.

Built for access

No sign-up or account. The interface is responsive and designed to work on phones and desktops.
